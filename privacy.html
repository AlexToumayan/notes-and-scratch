Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy covers Notes & Scratch for iPhone and Mac and its support website. Notes & Scratch is published by Alex Toumayan. You can use the app without creating an account.

Notes, code, and saved walkthroughs

The app reads and saves files in its local storage and in locations you give it access to. It remembers folder access and your default storage choice on your device. If you choose a folder managed by iCloud Drive or another storage provider, that provider handles syncing and backups under its own terms.

When you save a walkthrough, the app keeps a copy of the code, the recorded execution steps, and your step notes in its app storage. You can review it again on that device without sending it back to Python Tutor.

Dictation

When you start dictation, the app uses your microphone with your permission. Apple’s speech recognition processes the audio on your device. Apple may download the model needed for your language. The app saves the resulting text as part of your note when you insert it and keeps your remembered vocabulary and corrections in its local settings. The app does not save a recording of your dictation.

Code Trace and Python Tutor

When you select Run Trace, the app sends the current file’s code and its language setting to Python Tutor, an external service. Python Tutor runs the code on its servers and returns an execution trace for the app to display. The app does not send your note library or saved walkthrough annotations as part of a trace.

Python Tutor says it logs the code you visualize, the options you use, your interactions, and your IP address, and that it may analyze those logs for research. Its policy also describes the use of Google services for website analytics and advertising. Python Tutor manages its own logs. Python Tutor’s privacy policy

Code Trace is optional. You can keep a file out of Python Tutor by leaving it untraced. Remove passwords, API keys, confidential code, and personal information before running a trace.

Support website and email

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. GitHub Privacy Statement

If you email support, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you attach. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot, and follow up. We keep it for as long as needed for those purposes or as required by law. The mailbox is hosted by Gmail. Google Privacy Policy

Your privacy choices

  • Manage or delete your note and code files in the folder where you saved them. For a synced folder, check the provider’s trash and backup settings as well.
  • Turn off microphone access for Notes & Scratch in your device’s privacy settings. You can edit or clear saved corrections in Dictation Vocabulary.
  • To ask for access to or deletion of information you emailed us, contact kuroot.support@gmail.com. For information already sent to Python Tutor, contact Python Tutor directly.
